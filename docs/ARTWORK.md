# Artwork and concept origins

The two game illustrations establish the visual direction. The remaining five images preserve the flight and invention discussion that led into the game.

All are concept illustrations. Embedded labels, equations, dimensions, and performance claims on an image are not evidence of real equipment performance. Current scene inputs come from the [v0.3 playtest packet](UI-Worlds-Playtest-v0.3.md), with fictional mechanisms and conditional calculations identified there.

The supplied legacy boards have not been reconciled into a single real-world design. In particular, they do not establish a free-air thrust rating for the game’s pressure-cushion skimmer.
