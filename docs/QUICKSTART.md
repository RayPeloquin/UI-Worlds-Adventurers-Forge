# The Broken Skybridge — quickstart

Use the [v0.3 packet](UI-Worlds-Playtest-v0.3.md) as the full rule reference. This summary does not introduce a new scene variant.

## Table setup

A human referee runs four adventurers, shared among one to four players. Assign the ready characters in packet section 5 and one familiar specialty per adventurer. Begin in Story mode. Keep the public clock and inventory visible and owner-private handouts separate.

## Opening situation

The main bridge has a **60 ft / 18.288 m gap**. The party has **12 minutes: 24 beats of 30 seconds** before a dragon reaches the ledge. Arrival begins a new encounter rather than automatic defeat.

The merchant’s skimmer has no supported open-gap operating point. Original masonry anchors are cracked and their capacity is unknown. A separate maintenance cable crossing and a marked footpath are visible from the start.

**Starting resources:** 8 coins, 8 tagged stock units, and 30 electrical energy units. One energy unit is 10 kJ. Utility rope: 80 ft / 24.384 m; two unrated pitons and basic tools. Full crossing load: 600 kg, including gear and carriage. These are fixed fictional scene inputs.

## Offer choices

| Approach | Stated cost or limit |
| --- | --- |
| Footpath | 8 beats; leave bulky salvage worth 4 stock units. |
| Maintenance crew | 6 coins and 7 beats; preserves the full load. |
| Operate the existing crossing | Inspect 2 beats; rent for 2 coins and prepare 2 beats; on successful setup, cross in 2 beats and spend 10 energy units. |
| Main bridge repair | Missing structural materials, processes, and time; reassess if those change. |
| Skimmer investigation or another proposal | State the test objective, decisive unknown, resources, and consequences before commitment. |

Negotiation, distraction, retreat, and unexpected proposals remain available. Apply the same scene facts to every approach.

## Resolve one action

1. Player describes an intention.
2. Referee gives a short feasibility summary: supported, conditional, insufficient information, or outside current limits.
3. Agree on time, materials, energy, and possible consequences.
4. Roll only for eligible uncertain execution. For crossing setup, roll d20 + Craft against 12 after inspection supports use.
5. Record the result and resource changes together.

The crossing’s setup outcomes are in packet section 11. A failed unpowered setup spends inspection/preparation time and rental, releases the energy reservation, and sends nobody over the gap. A clean successful route spends six beats, two coins, and ten energy units in total.

## Familiars and appeals

Use Hint → Explain → Calculate → Challenge. A formal challenge names the ruling and supporting evidence. The referee may uphold it, partly uphold it, or explain why the ruling stands. Missing information can remain unresolved. Log the decision and its scope; discussion time is not fictional construction time.

## End the scene

Complete the [campaign journal](../templates/campaign-journal.md) and [playtest feedback](../templates/playtest-feedback.md). Update only the claims supported by the fictional observations. Keep real-world evidence unchanged by a campaign outcome.
