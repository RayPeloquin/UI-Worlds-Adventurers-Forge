# Validation status

## Design and calculations

The v0.3 packet is a consolidated tabletop playtest candidate. The conditional cushion, central-point cable, winch energy, and heat-engine examples were desk-checked during preparation. Their assumptions and limitations remain in the packet. A numerical check does not establish the fictional sources or validate the real hardware.

The v0.3 revision preserves those parameter sets and arithmetic, clarifies the platform’s diameter, and makes resource reservation distinct from expenditure.

## Publication integrity

Before publication, the package was checked for:

- Resolution of relative Markdown file and image links.
- Presence and PNG decodability of all seven artwork files.
- Agreement between original image bytes and the sizes and hashes in the artwork manifest.
- Byte-for-byte preservation of the current v0.3 and archived v0.2 packets.
- A complete README, quickstart, LinkedIn explainer, gallery, and paper-playtest templates.

## Work not yet performed

- A completed human tabletop playtest.
- A running game application or integrated AI companion service.
- Enforced familiar permissions, adversarial access tests, or automatic persistence.
- Hardware experiments or real-world validation of the concept boards.
- A completed D&D/SRD adapter or an established uniqueness claim.

The [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) identifies the next evidence needed. Actual playtest reports should name participants or roles, rules version, actions, outcomes, and observed problems without treating a scripted illustration as a measurement.
