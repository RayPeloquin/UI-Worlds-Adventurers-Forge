# U.I. Worlds — The Adventurer’s Forge

**An AI-assisted tabletop RPG concept where exploration, invention, and technical reasoning become part of the adventure.**

![Adventurers exploring U.I. Worlds](images/ui-worlds-cover.png)

**Current release: v0.3 tabletop playtest candidate.** The rules and opening scene are written; example calculations have been desk-checked. Human playtesting and software implementation are still ahead. Companion permissions and automatic saving are design requirements.

**Project concept and direction: Ray Peloquin.** Developed with AI assistance and iterative review through the Synthetic Court.

## The idea

Your party reaches a broken bridge. Something enormous is approaching through the mountain. A merchant offers a levitating platform—but its lift assumes a nearby supporting surface.

Investigate it, negotiate, hire the maintenance crew, operate an existing cable crossing, or take the footpath. Propose another approach and examine its requirements. Humans make the choices.

**Dice resolve uncertain execution within established limits.** A natural 20 can mean superb workmanship or spotting a flaw. Missing energy, materials, or manufacturing capability still needs a solution. Magic has explicit capabilities, costs, and limits.

## Four systems, plus manufacturing capacity

| System | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| U.I. Game Master | World, characters, mysteries, time, and consequences. |
| Physics Referee | A plain-language assessment before commitment, including decisive limits and unknowns. |
| Technology Compendium | Versioned designs, assumptions, calculations, tests, and failure records. |
| Campaign Journal | Observations, inventory, relationships, maps, and agreed rulings. |
| Manufacturing capacity | The tools, materials, processes, labor, and time actually available. |

## Every adventurer has a familiar

Choose a Cartographer, Engineer, Scholar, Naturalist, or Quartermaster. The proposed companions help map, explain, calculate, and preserve notes using their adventurer’s observations and authorized shared information.

They can formally challenge a referee ruling with a source, observation, calculation, or contradiction. The answer and its scope become a recorded precedent. The human table retains control.

A **Story / Workshop / Lab** dial changes how much technical detail players see. The underlying constraints remain consistent. Core ruling quality and access to decisive evidence remain equal across familiar options.

## Evidence stays scoped

The compendium separates a device’s physical or fictional basis, its campaign history, and evidence about specific real-world claims. An in-game success never validates real hardware. A field modification creates a new configuration whose evidence must be reviewed.

The proposed familiar privacy boundary requires host-enforced authorization. For the paper playtest, a human referee manages separate handouts. A shared chat containing secrets cannot provide isolated familiar knowledge merely by changing personalities.
