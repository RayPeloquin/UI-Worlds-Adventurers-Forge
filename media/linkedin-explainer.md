Your party reaches a broken bridge. Something enormous is approaching through the mountain. A merchant offers a levitating platform.

Your brass owl reads the technical notes.

“The lift requires a surface beneath it. We should discuss the canyon.” 🦉

Welcome to **U.I. Worlds — The Adventurer’s Forge**, an AI-assisted tabletop RPG concept where exploration, invention, and technical reasoning become part of the adventure.

The central rule:

**Dice resolve uncertain execution within the world’s established limits.**

A natural 20 can mean superb workmanship or spotting a flaw in time. It cannot create missing energy, materials, or equipment. Magic has declared capabilities, costs, and limits that remain consistent.

Four connected systems support play:

• **U.I. Game Master:** Runs the world, characters, mysteries, and consequences. Humans choose their adventurers’ actions.

• **Physics Referee:** Explains the decisive constraint before players commit resources. Sometimes the answer is “we need more information.”

• **Technology Compendium:** Records designs, assumptions, calculations, experiments, and failures. Campaign achievements stay separate from real-world engineering evidence.

• **Campaign Journal:** Tracks discoveries, inventory, relationships, and agreed rulings.

Manufacturing capacity matters too: understanding a design and having the tools to build it are separate achievements.

Each adventurer gets a specialized AI familiar: Cartographer, Engineer, Scholar, Naturalist, or Quartermaster. The proposed companions work from their character’s observations and authorized shared information, helping players map, investigate, calculate, and keep technical notes.

They can also formally challenge the referee—with a calculation, source, observation, or contradiction. A successful challenge changes the ruling and leaves a record.

Players choose how much detail they want through **Story, Workshop, or Lab** presentation. The underlying constraints stay consistent.

Invention is optional. Negotiate, investigate, hire help, take another route—or build something clever with what you actually have.

**Current stage:** v0.3 tabletop playtest draft, including the opening encounter, *The Broken Skybridge*. First playtesting and software implementation are ahead.

And yes, we gave the brass owl a formal appeals process. That may have been politically unwise. 😂
